## Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Capo Verde, focusing on relaxation and exploring the island's natural beauty.

## Travel Itinerary

| Day    | Activity           | Location          |
|--------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Day 1**| **Arrival in Sal** | **Milan to Sal**  |
|        | Flight from Milan to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID), Sal. Transfer to accommodation. Relax and settle in. | [Flight details](https://example.com/flights) |
| **Day 2**| **Beach Day & Santa Maria** | **Santa Maria**   |
|        | Enjoy the beautiful beaches of Santa Maria. Explore the town, pier, and local markets. | [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) |
| **Day 3**| **Island Tour: Salinas & Blue Eye** | **Sal Island**    |
|        | Visit the Salinas de Pedra de Lume (salt pans) and experience the "Blue Eye" at Buracona. | [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salinas_de_Pedra_de_Lume), [Buracona Blue Eye](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/buracona-blue-eye) |
| **Day 4**| **Water Sports or Relaxation** | **Santa Maria**   |
|        | Choose from activities like snorkeling, diving, windsurfing, or simply relax by the pool/beach. | [Water Sports in Sal](https://example.com/watersports) |
| **Day 5**| **Espargos & Local Culture** | **Espargos**      |
|        | Explore Espargos, the capital of Sal, and learn about local culture. Visit the local market. | [Espargos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espargos) |
| **Day 6**| **Catamaran Cruise & Sunset** | **Santa Maria Coast** |
|        | Enjoy a catamaran cruise along the coast, with opportunities for swimming and a beautiful sunset view. | [Catamaran Cruises Sal](https://example.com/catamaran) |
| **Day 7**| **Departure**      | **Sal to Milan**  |
|        | Enjoy a final breakfast. Transfer to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) for your flight back to Milan. | [Flight details](https://example.com/flights) |

Flights from Milan to Sal. Transfers and island tours will be arranged via local transport or rented car.

## Notes
*   **Visa:** Please check visa requirements for Capo Verde based on your nationality.
*   **Currency:** Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE). Euros are widely accepted in tourist areas.
*   **Language:** Portuguese and Cape Verdean Creole. English is spoken in tourist areas.
*   **Weather:** Expect warm and sunny weather in November.
