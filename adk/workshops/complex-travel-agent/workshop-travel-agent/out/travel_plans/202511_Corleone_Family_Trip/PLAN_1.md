
### Synoptic Overview
A 7-day escape for 2 adults to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, departing from Milan. This plan focuses on relaxation, exploring the island's natural beauty, and enjoying local cuisine.

### Travel Plan Details

| Day | Activity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Departure from Milan, arrival in Sal, hotel check-in | [Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en), [Amilcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://www.aeroportos.cv/index.php/eng/airports/sal) |
| **Day 2** | Relax at Santa Maria Beach, explore Santa Maria town | [Santa Maria Beach](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cabo-verde/sal/santa-maria) |
| **Day 3** | Excursion to Buracona Blue Eye and Pedra de Lume Salt Pans | [Buracona Blue Eye](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/buracona-blue-eye), [Pedra de Lume Salt Pans](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/pedra-de-lume-salt-pans) |
| **Day 4** | Catamaran cruise along the coast | [Santa Maria](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cabo-verde/sal/santa-maria) |
| **Day 5** | Kite surfing lessons or relaxation | [Kite Beach](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cabo-verde/sal/activities/kiteboarding-windsurfing/a/pa-act/v-2003889/1327117) |
| **Day 6** | Explore Palmeira fishing village, enjoy a seafood dinner | [Palmeira](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cabo-verde/sal/palmeira) |
| **Day 7** | Departure from Sal, arrival in Milan | [Amilcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://www.aeroportos.cv/index.php/eng/airports/sal), [Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en) |
