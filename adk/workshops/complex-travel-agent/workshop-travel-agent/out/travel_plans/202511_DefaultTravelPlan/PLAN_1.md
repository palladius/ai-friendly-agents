
## Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Capo Verde, focusing on relaxation, beautiful beaches, and local culture.

## Itinerary

| Day | Activity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | **Travel to Sal**: Flight from Milan to Sal, check into accommodation | [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde) |
| Day 2 | **Beach Day**: Relax at Santa Maria Beach | [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) |
| Day 3 | **Island Tour**: Explore the salt pans of Pedra de Lume, Buracona Blue Eye | [Pedra de Lume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedra_de_Lume) |
| Day 4 | **Water Sports**: Kitesurfing or windsurfing lessons | [Kite Beach](https://www.google.com/search?q=kitesurfing+sal+cape+verde) |
| Day 5 | **Local Culture**: Visit Santa Maria town, local markets | [Santa Maria, Cape Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) |
| Day 6 | **Catamaran Cruise**: Explore the coast, snorkeling | [Catamaran Tours Sal](https://www.google.com/search?q=catamaran+tours+sal+cape+verde) |
| Day 7 | **Departure**: Enjoy a final breakfast, transfer to airport for flight back to Milan | [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde) |
