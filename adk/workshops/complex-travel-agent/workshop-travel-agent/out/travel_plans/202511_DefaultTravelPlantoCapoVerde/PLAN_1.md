Synoptic Overview:
This plan outlines a relaxing 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, from November 15th to November 22nd, 2025. The focus is on enjoying the sun, beaches, and local culture.

Travel Plan: Milan to Sal, Capo Verde (November 15, 2025 - November 22, 2025)

**Day 1: Arrival in Sal**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Departure from [Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en).
*   **Late Afternoon/Evening:** Arrival at [Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://www.aeroportos.cv/index.php/en/sal-sid). Transfer to accommodation in Santa Maria.
*   **Evening:** Check-in and relax. Dinner at a local restaurant in Santa Maria.

**Day 2-6: Explore Sal**
*   **Activities:**
    *   Relax on the [beaches of Santa Maria](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1188506-d2466981-Reviews-Praia_de_Santa_Maria-Santa_Maria_Ilha_do_Sal.html).
    *   Visit [Buracona - The Blue Eye](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g482847-d1576201-Reviews-Buracona_The_Blue_Eye-Ilha_do_Sal.html) and Pedra de Lume Salt Pans.
    *   Experience water sports: Kitesurfing, windsurfing, or diving (lessons available locally).
    *   Explore [Santa Maria Pier](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1188506-d12975871-Reviews-Santa_Maria_Pier-Santa_Maria_Ilha_do_Sal.html) and watch the local fishermen.
    *   Enjoy local cuisine at various restaurants.
    *   Consider a catamaran trip or island tour.

**Day 7: Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a final breakfast and some last-minute souvenir shopping.
*   **Afternoon:** Transfer to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) for your departure back to Milan.

**Accommodation (Suggestions in Santa Maria):**
*   [Hotel Morabeza](https://www.hotelmorabeza.com/)
*   [Meliá Dunas Beach Resort & Spa](https://www.melia.com/en/hotels/cape-verde/sal/melia-dunas-beach-resort-and-spa)
*   [Hilton Cabo Verde Sal Resort](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/sidcchi-hilton-cabo-verde-sal-resort/)

**Estimated Costs (for 2 adults, excluding flights):**
*   Accommodation: €800 - €1500 (7 nights, depending on choice)
*   Food & Activities: €500 - €800
*   Transfers: €50 - €100
