## Corleone Family Default Trip: Milan to Sal, Capo Verde

### Synoptic Overview
This plan outlines a relaxing trip for two adults from Milan, Italy, to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, for a week in mid-November. The focus is on leisure and enjoying the island's natural beauty.
