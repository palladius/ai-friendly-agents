## Synoptic Overview
A delightful week-long escape for Riccardo and Caterina from Milan to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, offering a blend of relaxation and adventure.

### Travel Details
| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Milan, Italy |
| **Destination** | Sal, Capo Verde |
| **Travelers** | 2 Adults (Riccardo & Caterina) |
| **Dates** | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22 |

### Accommodation
*   **Suggested Hotel:** [Hilton Cabo Verde Sal Resort](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/sidscpv-hilton-cabo-verde-sal-resort/)

### Activities & Excursions
*   **Relaxation:** Enjoy the pristine beaches of Santa Maria.
*   **Adventure:** Explore the salt pans of Pedra de Lume.
*   **Culture:** Visit the charming local markets.

### Transportation
*   **Flights:** Direct flights from Milan to Sal are often available. For more details, refer to [Fabio Volo - Flight Information](https://example.com/fabio_volo)
*   **On-Island:** Taxis and car rentals are readily available.

### Important Notes
*   Visa requirements for Capo Verde should be checked prior to travel.
*   The local currency is the Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE).
*   For hotel bookings, please consult [Barabba - Hotel Information](https://example.com/barabba)
