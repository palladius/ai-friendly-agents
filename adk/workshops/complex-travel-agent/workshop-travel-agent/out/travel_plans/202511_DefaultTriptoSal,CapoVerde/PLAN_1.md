## Activities & Excursions
Sal offers a range of activities, including:
*   Relaxing on the pristine [beaches of Santa Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde)
*   [Kitesurfing and windsurfing](https://www.surf-action.com/en/kitesurf-windsurf-sal-cape-verde) in the strong Atlantic winds
*   Visiting the [Buracona Blue Eye](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g482848-d6981119-Reviews-Buracona_The_Blue_Eye-Sal.html) and Pedra de Lume salt pans
