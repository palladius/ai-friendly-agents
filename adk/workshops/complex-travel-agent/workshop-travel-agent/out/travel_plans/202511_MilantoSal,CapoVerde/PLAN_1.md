
### Synoptic Overview
This plan outlines a 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Capo Verde, focusing on relaxation and exploring the island's natural beauty.

| Destination | Dates | Travelers | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milan to Sal, Capo Verde | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22 | 2 Adults | Beaches, Watersports, Local Culture |

### Detailed Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Sal & Santa Maria**
*   **Morning:** Departure from [Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en) to [Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://www.aeroportos.cv/en/airports/sal-international-airport/).
*   **Afternoon:** Arrive in Sal, transfer to accommodation in [Santa Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde).
*   **Evening:** Relax on Santa Maria Beach, enjoy dinner at a local restaurant.

**Day 2: Santa Maria Beach & Watersports**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy the main beach of Santa Maria.
*   **Afternoon:** [Watersports activities](https://www.capeverde.com/activities/water-sports) such as windsurfing, kitesurfing, or snorkeling.
*   **Evening:** Explore the Santa Maria pier, watch the local fishermen.

**Day 3: Island Tour - Sal**
*   **Full Day:** [Island tour](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Products-g482847-zfg11867-Sal.html) including:
    *   [Buracona Lagoon & Blue Eye](https://www.capeverde.com/highlights/buracona-blue-eye).
    *   [Pedra de Lume Salt Pans](https://www.capeverde.com/highlights/pedra-de-lume-salt-pans).
    *   [Shark Bay](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g482847-d15024476-Reviews-Shark_Bay_Beach-Sal.html).

**Day 4: Espargos & Local Culture**
*   **Morning:** Visit [Espargos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espargos), the capital of Sal, explore local markets and admire street art.
*   **Afternoon:** Learn about Cape Verdean culture, perhaps a cooking class or a visit to a local music venue.
*   **Evening:** Dinner in Espargos.

**Day 5: Relaxation or Catamaran Cruise**
*   **Option 1 (Relaxation):** Spend the day relaxing on the beach or by the pool.
*   **Option 2 (Excursion):** [Catamaran cruise](https://www.getyourguide.com/sal-island-l272/from-santa-maria-sal-island-half-day-catamaran-cruise-t424729/) along the coast of Sal, with swimming and snorkeling stops.

**Day 6: Quad Biking or Buggy Tour**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** [Quad biking](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g482847-d17469317-Quad_Bike_4x4_Adventure_from_Santa_Maria-Sal.html) or [buggy tour](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g482847-d17469317-Quad_Bike_4x4_Adventure_from_Santa_Maria-Sal.html) to explore the desert landscapes of Sal.
*   **Evening:** Farewell dinner in Santa Maria, enjoying the sunset.

**Day 7: Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a final breakfast in Santa Maria, last-minute souvenir shopping.
*   **Afternoon:** Transfer to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) for departure to Milan.

### Important Notes
*   **Visa:** Check [visa requirements for Cape Verde](https://www.capeverde.com/cape-verde-visa) based on your nationality.
*   **Currency:** [Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_escudo). Euros are widely accepted in tourist areas.
*   **Language:** Portuguese, but English and Italian are commonly spoken in tourist areas.
*   **Health:** Consult your doctor about recommended vaccinations.
*   **Travel Insurance:** Highly recommended.
