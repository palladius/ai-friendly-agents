# Synoptic Overview
A one-week escape to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, for two adults. Enjoy the pristine beaches, vibrant culture, and relaxing atmosphere.

## Travel Details

| Detail        | Description                                                                     |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Destination** | [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde)                |
| **Origin**      | Milan, Italy                                                                    |
| **Dates**       | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22                                                        |
| **Travelers**   | 2 Adults                                                                        |

## Accommodation Options
For accommodation, consider exploring options on [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/region/cv/sal.en-gb.html) or [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/sal-cape-verde/stays).

## Activities & Excursions
*   **Relax on the beaches:** Santa Maria Beach, Kite Beach
*   **Water Sports:** [Kitesurfing](https://www.kitebeaches.com/kitesurf/capeverde/sal/info.aspx), Windsurfing, Diving
*   **Explore Santa Maria:** Visit the pier, local markets, and restaurants.
*   **Day trips:** Discover the [Salt Mines of Pedra de Lume](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/salinas-de-pedra-de-lume), Buracona Blue Eye.

## Transportation
Flights can be searched on [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com/routes/mila/sid/milan-to-sal-caboverde.html). Once on the island, taxis and car rentals are available.

## Important Notes
*   **Visa:** Check visa requirements for Capo Verde based on your nationality.
*   **Currency:** Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE). Euros are also widely accepted.
*   **Language:** Portuguese, but English and Italian are commonly spoken in tourist areas.
