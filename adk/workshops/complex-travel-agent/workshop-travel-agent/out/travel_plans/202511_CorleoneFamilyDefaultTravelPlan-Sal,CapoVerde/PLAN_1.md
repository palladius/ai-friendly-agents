
## Corleone Family Default Travel Plan: Milan to Sal, Capo Verde

**Synoptic Overview:** A 7-day escape for 2 adults from Milan, Italy, to the beautiful island of Sal, Capo Verde, known for its stunning beaches and water sports.

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| **Destination** | Sal, Capo Verde |
| **Dates** | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22 |
| **Travelers** | 2 Adults |
| **Origin** | Milan, Italy |

### Suggested Activities & Attractions in Sal:

*   **Beaches:**
    *   [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) - Famous for its golden sands and clear waters.
    *   [Kite Beach](https://www.kitebeachsal.com/) - Ideal for kitesurfing and windsurfing enthusiasts.

*   **Natural Wonders:**
    *   [Buracona Lagoon & Blue Eye](https://www.capeverde.com/island/sal/buracona-blue-eye) - A natural pool with a mesmerizing "blue eye" effect at certain times of day.
    *   [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://www.capeverde.com/island/sal/salinas-de-pedra-de-lume) - Salt pans located within an extinct volcano crater, offering a unique floating experience.

*   **Culture & Towns:**
    *   [Espargos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espargos) - The capital of Sal, offering a glimpse into local life.
    *   [Santa Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) - A vibrant town with a pier, local market, and various restaurants.

### Dining Suggestions:

*   **Fresh Seafood:** Enjoy the daily catch at numerous restaurants along Santa Maria beach.
*   **Local Cuisine:** Try traditional Cape Verdean dishes like cachupa.
