**About Sal, Capo Verde:**
Sal is one of the islands of Cape Verde, known for its beautiful sandy beaches and vibrant culture. It's a popular destination for water sports like windsurfing and kitesurfing. Explore the main town of Espargos or relax in Santa Maria.

**Key Attractions:**
* [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde)
