
### Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Cape Verde, offering a blend of relaxation and adventure.

### Itinerary

| Day       | Activity                                   | Location             |
| :-------- | :----------------------------------------- | :------------------- |
| **Day 1** | Arrival in Sal, Transfer to Accommodation | Espargos, Sal        |
| **Day 2** | Explore Santa Maria Beach & Pier           | Santa Maria, Sal     |
| **Day 3** | Excursion to Buracona Blue Eye & Pedra de Lume Saltworks | Sal Island           |
| **Day 4** | Relax at Ponta Preta Beach, Water Sports   | Santa Maria, Sal     |
| **Day 5** | Catamaran Cruise or Island Tour            | Sal Coastline        |
| **Day 6** | Free Day for Leisure or Souvenir Shopping  | Santa Maria, Sal     |
| **Day 7** | Departure from Sal                         | Espargos, Sal        |
