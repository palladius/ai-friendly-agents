## Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Cape Verde, focusing on relaxation, beaches, and local culture.

## Itinerary

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | **Travel to Sal**: Flight from Milan to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID), Sal. Transfer to accommodation. |
| Day 2 | **Santa Maria Beach**: Relax on the white sands of Santa Maria Beach. [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde) |
| Day 3 | **Salt Mines of Pedra de Lume**: Explore the historic salt mines and float in the saline waters. [Pedra de Lume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedra_de_Lume) |
| Day 4 | **Catamaran Cruise**: Enjoy a catamaran cruise along the coast, often including snorkeling opportunities. |
| Day 5 | **Espargos & Local Culture**: Visit Espargos, the capital of Sal, to experience local life and cuisine. [Espargos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espargos) |
| Day 6 | **Buracona Blue Eye**: Discover the natural wonder of Buracona, including the "Blue Eye" cave. [Buracona](https://www.capeverde.com/island/sal/buracona-blue-eye) |
| Day 7 | **Departure**: Enjoy a final Cape Verdean breakfast before transferring to the airport for your flight back to Milan. |

## Accommodation
*   **Option 1**: [Hilton Cabo Verde Sal Resort](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/sidsihh-hilton-cabo-verde-sal-resort/)
*   **Option 2**: [Meliá Dunas Beach Resort & Spa](https://www.melia.com/en/hotels/cape-verde/sal/melia-dunas-beach-resort-and-spa)

## Flights
*   **Milan (MXP) to Sal (SID)**: [TAP Air Portugal](https://www.flytap.com/), [Neos](https://www.neosair.it/)|

## Transportation
*   Taxis and shared minibuses (collectivos) are readily available on Sal.
*   Car rental is an option for independent exploration.

## Notes
*   **Currency**: Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE). Euros are widely accepted in tourist areas.
*   **Language**: Portuguese and Cape Verdean Creole. English is spoken in tourist areas.
*   **Visa**: Check visa requirements for Italian citizens traveling to Cape Verde.
