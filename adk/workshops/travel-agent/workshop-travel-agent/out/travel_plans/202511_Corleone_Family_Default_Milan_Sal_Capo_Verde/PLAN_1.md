## Corleone Family - Milan to Sal, Capo Verde (2 Adults)

### Synoptic Overview
This plan outlines a relaxing 7-day trip to Sal, Capo Verde, departing from Milan. Enjoy pristine beaches, water sports, and local culture.

### Destination: Sal, Capo Verde
*   **Location:** [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde)
*   **Dates:** 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22
*   **Travelers:** 2 Adults
