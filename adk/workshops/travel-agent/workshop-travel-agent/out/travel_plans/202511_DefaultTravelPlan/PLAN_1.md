**Synoptic Overview:**
This plan outlines a 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Capo Verde, focusing on relaxation and exploring the island's natural beauty.

**Travel Details:**
| Detail          | Description                                                    |
| :-------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Destination** | [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde) |
| **Origin**      | [Milan, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan)              |
| **Travelers**   | 2 Adults                                                       |
| **Dates**       | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22                                       |

**Activities & Highlights:**
*   Relax on the beautiful beaches of Santa Maria.
*   Explore the salt pans of Pedra de Lume.
*   Visit Buracona (Blue Eye).
*   Enjoy water sports such as kitesurfing and windsurfing.
