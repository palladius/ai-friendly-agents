
### Synoptic Overview
A 7-day escape to the beautiful island of Sal, Cape Verde, for Riccardo and Caterina. Enjoy pristine beaches, vibrant culture, and relaxing resorts.

### Itinerary

| Day | Activity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival in Sal, Transfer to Hotel | Espargos, Santa Maria |
| 2 | Relax on Santa Maria Beach | Santa Maria |
| 3 | Explore Sal (e.g., Buracona Blue Eye, Pedra de Lume Salt Pans) | Island Tour |
| 4 | Catamaran Cruise | Santa Maria |
| 5 | Water Sports (Kitesurfing, Windsurfing) | Santa Maria |
| 6 | Leisure and Souvenir Shopping | Santa Maria |
| 7 | Departure from Sal | Espargos |
