
**Synoptic Overview**

This plan outlines a relaxing week-long trip for two adults to Sal, Capo Verde, departing from Milan. It includes flight and accommodation suggestions, along with optional activities to enhance your stay.

| Category      | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Destination** | [Sal, Capo Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal,_Cape_Verde)                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Travelers**   | 2 Adults                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Dates**       | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Departure**   | [Milan, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan)                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

**Detailed Plan**

*   **Flights:**
    *   **Outbound:** Milan (MXP) to Sal (SID) on 2025-11-15
    *   **Return:** Sal (SID) to Milan (MXP) on 2025-11-22
    *   *Consider airlines like [TAP Air Portugal](https://www.flytap.com/) or [Neos](https://www.neosair.it/en) for potential routes.*

*   **Accommodation:**
    *   *Explore options in Santa Maria, Sal, such as [Hilton Cabo Verde Sal Resort](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/sidsihh-hilton-cabo-verde-sal-resort/) or [Hotel Riu Palace Santa Maria](https://www.riu.com/en/hotel/cape-verde/sal/hotel-riu-palace-santa-maria/).*

*   **Activities:**
    *   Relax on the beautiful beaches of Santa Maria.
    *   Try water sports like kitesurfing or windsurfing.
    *   Visit the [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedra_de_Lume).
    *   Explore the charming town of Santa Maria.
