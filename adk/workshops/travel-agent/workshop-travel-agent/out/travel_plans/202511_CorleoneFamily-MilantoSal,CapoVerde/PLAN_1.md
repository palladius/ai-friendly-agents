**Synoptic Overview:**
*   **Destination:** Sal, Capo Verde
*   **Origin:** Milan, Italy
*   **Travelers:** 2 Adults
*   **Dates:** November 15, 2025 - November 22, 2025

**Proposed Itinerary:**

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | **Travel from Milan to Sal:** Departure from [Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en) to [Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://www.aeroportos.cv/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=46&Itemid=162&lang=en). Upon arrival, transfer to accommodation. |
| Day 2-6 | **Explore Sal Island:** Enjoy the beaches, water sports, and local culture. Consider visiting [Santa Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Cape_Verde), [Buracona - Olho Azul](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/buracona-blue-eye), and the [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedra_de_Lume). |
| Day 7 | **Leisure Day:** Relax at the resort, last-minute souvenir shopping, or enjoy a final Cape Verdean meal. |
| Day 8 | **Departure:** Transfer from accommodation to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) for your flight back to Milan. |
