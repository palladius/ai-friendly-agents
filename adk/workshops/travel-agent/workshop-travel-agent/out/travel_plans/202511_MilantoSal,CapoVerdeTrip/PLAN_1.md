## Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Cape Verde, focusing on relaxation and exploring the island's natural beauty.

## Itinerary

| Day | Activity | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1: Arrival in Sal** | Flight from [Milan Malpensa (MXP)](https://www.milanomalpensa-airport.com/en) to [Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Am%C3%ADlcar_Cabral_International_Airport). Transfer to accommodation in [Santa Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Sal). | Sal, Cape Verde |
| **Day 2: Santa Maria Beach & Pier** | Relax on [Santa Maria Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria,_Sal) and visit the vibrant [Santa Maria Pier](https://www.capeverde.com/islands/sal/santa-maria-pier/). | Santa Maria, Sal |
| **Day 3: Island Tour: Buracona & Salinas** | Explore the natural pool of [Buracona - Blue Eye](https://www.capeverde.com/islands/sal/buracona-blue-eye/) and the salt pans of [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://www.capeverde.com/islands/sal/salinas-de-pedra-de-lume/). | Sal, Cape Verde |
| **Day 4: Catamaran Cruise** | Enjoy a catamaran cruise along the coast, often including swimming and snorkeling opportunities. | Off the coast of Sal |
| **Day 5: Espargos & Local Culture** | Visit [Espargos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espargos), the capital of Sal, for a taste of local life and markets. | Espargos, Sal |
| **Day 6: Relaxation & Souvenirs** | Free day for relaxation, last-minute souvenir shopping, or optional activities like surfing lessons. | Santa Maria, Sal |
| **Day 7: Departure** | Transfer to Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) for your flight back to Milan. | Sal, Cape Verde |

## Important Notes
*   Visa requirements: Please check the latest visa regulations for Cape Verde based on your nationality.
*   Currency: [Cape Verdean Escudo (CVE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_escudo). Euros are also widely accepted in tourist areas.
*   Language: Portuguese (official), Cape Verdean Creole.
