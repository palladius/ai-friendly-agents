
### Synoptic Overview
A 7-day trip for 2 adults from Milan, Italy to Sal, Capo Verde, focusing on relaxation and exploring the island's natural beauty.

| Destination     | Dates         | Travelers | Accommodation | Activities                         |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------|---------------|------------------------------------|
| Milan to Sal, Capo Verde | 2025-11-15 to 2025-11-22 | 2 Adults  | [Hotel Riu Palace Santa Maria](https://www.riu.com/en/hotel/cape-verde/sal/hotel-riu-palace-santa-maria/) | Beach relaxation, watersports, island tours |

### Detailed Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Sal & Relaxation**
*   **Morning/Afternoon**: Arrive at Sal International Airport (SID). Transfer to your accommodation.
*   **Evening**: Settle in and enjoy a relaxing dinner at the hotel.

**Day 2-6: Explore Sal**
*   **Activities**:
    *   [Santa Maria Beach](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g482845-d677119-Reviews-Santa_Maria_Beach-Santa_Maria_Ilha_do_Sal.html): Enjoy the pristine white sands and clear waters.
    *   [Buracona - Blue Eye Cave](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/buracona-blue-eye-cave): Witness the natural phenomenon of the "Blue Eye" (best seen between 10:30 AM and 1:00 PM).
    *   [Salinas de Pedra de Lume](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/salinas-de-pedra-de-lume): Float in the therapeutic salt lakes within an ancient volcano crater.
    *   [Shark Bay (Pontinha Preta)](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g482845-d15024227-Reviews-Shark_Bay-Santa_Maria_Ilha_do_Sal.html): Observe lemon sharks in their natural habitat.
    *   Watersports: [Kiteboarding and windsurfing](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/cape-verde/sal/activities/windsurfing-kitesurfing) are very popular.

**Day 7: Departure**
*   **Morning**: Enjoy a final breakfast in Sal.
*   **Afternoon**: Transfer to Sal International Airport (SID) for your departure.
